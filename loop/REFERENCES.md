# Reference set: GTA V quality bar

Built 2026-10-22T06:44:01Z for the Orchard Reach Opus 5.5 loop (wave 05). The images live only in `loop/references/` (git-ignored, see `.gitignore` `/loop/references/`). They are **Rockstar Games copyright publicity material**, used here only as a visual quality bar for critics. Never copy them, crop them, trace them or put them into the game, `progress/`, docs or any commit. This file holds links only.

**Source rule:** every GTAV-xx image was downloaded from Rockstar's own sites/CDN only: Rockstar Newswire articles on rockstargames.com (their media links point at `media.rockstargames.com`, which is served by the same Akamai host `media-rockstargames-com.akamaized.net` used below) or assets bundled into the official rockstargames.com GTA V web module. No fan sites, wikis or trailers were used. No video frames were extracted.

**Verification:** each file was checked with `sips -g pixelWidth -g pixelHeight` (all decode as JPEG at the listed size) and visually inspected (Read tool) before tagging. Camera height/distance values are visual estimates from the frame, not measurements.

**Caveats:** many 2013-2015 press screens carry a "grand theft auto V" logo watermark; several are publicity-grade captures (Rockstar Editor/photo-mode framing, colour grading), not raw gameplay frames. GTAV-17, 24, 33 and 38 come from the 2025 PC upgrade (ray-traced) posts. GTAV-33..38 are GTA Online promo art (same game/engine). Close-ups and low hero-car angles are "beauty" framings: compare our build at matching camera scale, not against the gameplay cameras alone.
