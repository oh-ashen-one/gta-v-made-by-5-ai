# Real source-facing window/reveal contract

`source_facade_bays_v10.py` takes a source-oriented floor and a rectangular perimeter room, then rays its actual outer edge toward the source facade. It splits intervals at actual source vertices and emits precise edge/u positions for future glazing. No title, texture or picture of a window substitutes for that geometry.

Each bay carries separate source-conforming **floor**, **ceiling**, and **2m-headroom** contour polygons. The outer tower leans, so these contours can differ. The factory must build actual support, side reveals and sloped outside glazing. The standing envelope must use actual available headroom; do not register the whole floor contour as a uniform3.08m dry box. Source glass collision remains a real barrier. Window descriptors are not permission to cut an unguarded hole through the building.

The helper refuses bays that cross another room, the core, public circulation or an atrium void. It bounds a simple reveal to2m; a larger missing connection requires a real furnished extension or a different layout. It also refuses already expanded/annex unions it cannot safely adapt, rather than laying duplicate support over a prior room. A future arbitrary-polygon adapter must use the actual outer union boundary.

Six bounded CPU tests passed: real tapered bay, changed support refusal, foreign-room occlusion, public-route occlusion, exact source edge parameters, and truthful incomplete-fixture scope. The retained historical perimeter fixture has1,543 placed rooms; all have at least2.4m of candidate source frontage after real reveals. **This is not the new1,844-key catalogue allocation**, not every bedroom/living sightline, and not installed/native/rendered glazing.

Root integration needs the final room allocation, source frame/datum, explicit facade material/collision ownership and actual aperture/window constructor. It must preserve the source outer/taper and prove native floor support, head clearance, daylight and sightlines with real runtime captures before accepting those rooms.
