**Largest actionable gap — P1 for visual acceptance: correct tree morphology.** Arup p22 shows open branching trunks and broad, irregular crowns above tall vessels. R5's repeated vertically stacked dark blobs read as ornamental topiary. Use licensed, branching tree assets at the established vessel scale. Same-camera test: the nearest trees in shot 03 should expose branch structure and spread their crowns at least twice their vessel-rim width, with varied silhouettes. [Primary reference](https://www.arup.com/globalassets/downloads/arup-journal/the-arup-journal-2012-issue-1.pdf)

**P2 — floor response.** In shot 02's fixed pixel rectangle `(800,520)-(1080,690)`, R5 mean RGB is `(255,248.3,213.4)` and luminance standard deviation is **1.38/255**. The surface visibly lacks stone grain and restrained reflections. Add subtle stone variation/joints and tune its light response; verify the same view preserves those details. Pale color is progress; brightness alone is insufficient.

**P2 — occupied edges.** Build coherent seating and reception frontage from source-backed public program; retain the clear promenade. Arup p20's layered planted/lounge edges are absent here. Historical restaurant fit-outs are not automatically current.

**Separate assembly gate:** the splayed opaque tower walls, balcony bands, roof relationship, public-street continuation and exterior setting remain unshown. Shot 04 is still a platform facing blue. These are integration omissions, not proof of incorrect source-world data or a local module defect. Require an integrated eye-level atrium view before whole-scene acceptance; the staged glass enclosure cannot establish MBS identity by itself.
