## Image-grounded problem and selected changes

All four actual R4 stills in `/private/tmp/singapore-fidelity/mbs-public-l1-r4/stills/` were viewed. The source renders on Arup 2012 pages 20–22 were viewed privately. R4 has knee-high grass boxes, sparse outdoor benches, an unarticulated brown counter, gold text against blue glazing and an amber reflective tile grid. The primary photos show a pale promenade, tall tree vessels/crowns and richly occupied ground-level edges. Tower shells, balconies, surrounding city and real public entry assembly remain root integration scope.

The same 25 furniture items remain: 12 benches, 12 planters and one reception. Planter footprint remains 1.8 x 1.8 m; each solid full-height box now extends to 3 m. Generic vessel geometry adapts the existing original Kit primitives, with a simple plain taper/rim/base, not a traced or copied artwork mesh. Each vessel reuses the original downtown `tembusu` mesh/shader from `game/src/world/downtown/canopy.gd`. Mesh extents are normalized conservatively around its true trunk origin so the visible trunk and upper solid stem share a center. Tree heights above the vessel are staged at 4.5/5.6/6.2 m for T3/T2/T1, with desired crown widths 2.1/3.8/4.8 m. Crown width shrinks against the full proposed footprint, top-of-crown inclined wall and end caps. Those exact tree dimensions and locations are assumptions, not surveyed MBS fit-out.

The floor uses a module-local pale stone shader with metre-scaled 2.4 x 1.2 m quiet slabs, small joints, subtle grain, roughness .64–.685, zero metal and restrained specular .28. It does not stretch the existing warm tile texture over the long quads. Existing exterior/cache materials are not changed. Existing original benches receive seat pads wholly inside their existing full physical box. Reception receives layered stone/wood/bronze surfaces and small original terminal details, retaining its original physical box. High-contrast generic wayfinding has a solid backing, larger lettering and a wall/desk position instead of tiny unsupported gold labels. Door leaves are visibly glazed, framed and parked at ninety degrees outside the configured opening, with full solid leaf collision, handles and a flush visual sill; the real aperture stays clear.

## Clearance and verification limits

It reports 25 furniture items, minimum centerline-to-inner-edge 2.5 m (4 m clear spine plus .5 m buffer on each side), minimum full-height furniture wall clearance .544444 m, twelve crowns and minimum overhead crown-wall clearance .18 m. This is **offline Python arithmetic, not Godot physics or rendered acceptance**. The audit JSON records the exact module/reference hashes.

All pre-existing actual production-capsule/path/join checks remain. Added assertions check the 12 raised solid vessels and upper stem boxes, their lateral containment in the vessel footprint and both off-center full-width doorway capsule paths. No public-stage camera, lighting fixture, route, movement or acceptance flag was weakened. No engine result has yet been observed for R5.
