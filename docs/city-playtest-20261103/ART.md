# Compact GPS interface and visual controls

Owner superseded the large green HUD and weapon wheel with a compact light GPS map, one top-right objective, a small E prompt, and one current-weapon card selected with 1/2. Existing generated gun raster art is reused at a smaller size; the detailed map remains live cartography from actual roads and building footprints.

The controls-manual PNG was created with the built-in image generator, then copied unchanged into `game/assets/ui/generated/controls-manual.png` and bound to the pause screen. It uses six illustrated tiles with keyboard/mouse controls, rather than the old prose help panel.

Generation brief: professional landscape 3:2 Orchard Reach visual manual, cream white background, charcoal typography, vivid blue accents, miniature realistic 3D illustrations, six tiles for WASD/Shift movement, Space jump, WASD/Shift driving drift, RMB window/aim and LMB fire plus R reload, M map/waypoint, E vehicle entry/exit and helicopter Space climb/Shift descend; bottom 1 pistol/2 SMG and mouse look. Generous whitespace, readable keycaps, no prose paragraphs.

Source-first exception: the owner explicitly requested built-in image generation for this custom instruction manual. No new gameplay character, vehicle or animation was AI-generated in this pass.
