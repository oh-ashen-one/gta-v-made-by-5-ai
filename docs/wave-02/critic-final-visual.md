The **on-foot concept** uses several depth layers in a short distance: recessed arcade, people and storefront objects, kerb/drain detail, shaded road, busy hawker frontage, then housing and skyline. `photo_08_street_arcade.png` has a useful arcade silhouette but substitutes a very long repeated row, sparse walkers, almost no near-field street dressing and a broad empty road. The **driving concept** surrounds a clearly integrated car with layered planting, traffic, local frontage and a water/bridge reveal. Soak `06`, `18` and `29` instead expose straight roads, repeated architecture and long bare surfaces. `28_car_entered.png` additionally shows a broad wavy specular patch across asphalt that reads closer to a glossy synthetic surface than convincing dry road aggregate.

The **helicopter concept** is a continuous, richly occupied city with varied green canopy, roof/service detail, waterfront development and layered skyline. `photo_06_aerial_district.png` and `photo_07_aerial_waterfront.png` expose the regular block template and extensive paving. Soak `09`/`21_aerial_view.png` reveal individually spaced towers on a nearly bare rectangular far-shore plane, calm featureless water and visibly terminated roadway-like strips. Background scenery may be inaccessible; its visible composition still needs to sell a city.

Forensic one-frame judgment: clearly prototype output. The main tells are copied module cadence, sparse ground use, thin repeated tree crowns, abstractly mottled surfaces, empty flat-colored interiors and detached car fragments. This is not an uncertain shipped-game/prototype distinction.
