## Blocking acceptance findings

1. **The 1800-second floor is not enforced.** `capture_director.gd:73–74` clamps the supplied `--soak-seconds` to 30–1800, and `:451–456` accepts that shortened value as the completion contract. With three missions and the distance gates completed, a 900- or 1200-second run can return success. `tests/soak-bound-negative` verifies only that a 30-second run with zero completions fails; its interpretation does not prove the fixed 30-minute floor. Keep a short smoke deadline, but prevent an acceptance result unless actual monotonic elapsed time is at least 1800 seconds and the requested acceptance contract is the fixed workload. Add a negative predicate test where all other conditions are satisfied below 1800 seconds.

2. **Accumulated distances can conceal a later free-roam softlock or broken aircraft lifecycle.** `:147–150` rejects death/disabled transport only during `mission`; `:151–154` ends the soak immediately at its deadline; `:451–456` checks cumulative distance but no current usability/recovery state. `:423`, `:434`, and `:445–447` call timeout-based helpers without asserting arrival, intact landing, successful exit, or successful on-foot recovery after each completed activity. Once 100 m foot / 200 m car / 200 m aircraft have accrued, a later navigation/landing failure still permits success at the deadline. Require logged successful mixed-activity cycles with bounded progress/recovery, an explicit final usable-control state, and no unresolved failures. If death is a supported free-roam recovery case, record its recovery rather than silently ignoring it. Fixture relocation itself is currently excluded correctly because `_free_last_actor` resets on entering free roam.

3. **The inspected instruments cannot support the memory/error portions of QA-020.** `:158–165` samples gameplay state only, and `:770–794` writes frame summaries without memory/VRAM/object-count trends. `game/tools/bounded_run.py:20–38` records identity and terminal status but no memory samples. Add a task-owned external RSS sampler and useful engine counters, or explicitly leave leak/memory-trend acceptance NOT TESTED. The wrapper's fatal pattern at `bounded_run.py:34` also omits generic `ERROR:` lines: `soak-bound-negative/engine.log:3` contains one while `process_result.json` reports `fatal_errors: []`. This specific OS-status error is not proof of a gameplay defect, but all runtime errors should be retained and classified; an empty narrow fatal list cannot mean error-free execution.
